Privacy Policy

Mall Chase · a QtMesh Games title by Fernando Tonon · Effective 6 October 2026

In short: Mall Chase does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal information. It has no ads, no analytics, no accounts and no tracking. Your progress is saved only on your own device.

Who we are

Mall Chase is an arcade game developed by Fernando Tonon and published as part of the QtMesh Games collection. This policy applies to every version of the game: Android, desktop (including through the QtMesh Games launcher and Steam) and the web version at fernandotonon.github.io/mall-chase-web.

Information we collect

None. The game does not ask for, collect or transmit personal information such as your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or advertising identifiers.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the game keeps a small save file on your device containing:

  • your high score and best score per stage,
  • which stages you have unlocked and which in-game achievements you have earned,
  • your settings (sound, fullscreen, camera shake).

This file never leaves your device and is not linked to you. You can delete it at any time by uninstalling the app (Android), clearing the app's storage, or clearing the site data for the web version in your browser.

Permissions

The Android app requests no special permissions: no internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage access beyond its own private save file.

Network use

  • Android and desktop: the game does not make any internet connections.
  • QtMesh Games launcher / Steam (desktop only): when the game is started from the QtMesh Games launcher, it talks to the launcher over a local connection on your own computer only to report achievements and game statistics, which the launcher may forward to Steam. That data is handled by Valve under the Steam Privacy Policy.
  • Web version: the game files are delivered by GitHub Pages. GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address when serving the files, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. The game itself sends nothing back.

Third-party services

Mall Chase contains no third-party advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or social SDKs.

Children

Mall Chase is suitable for all ages. Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
